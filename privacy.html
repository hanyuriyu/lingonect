Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 23 November 2026

1. Who Is Responsible

Lingonect® is run by H Ben, an individual based in Spain (European Union), who is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: contact@lingonect.com

Lingonect® is designed to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what we process, why, on what legal basis, for how long, who else handles it, and your rights.

2. What We Collect and Why

Account

  • Your email address, how you sign in (Google, Apple or email and password), and the name your Google or Apple account provides
  • Used to sign you in, run the invite-only access and contact you about your access
  • Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b))
  • Kept until you delete your account

Access requests

  • Your email address, name, sign-in method, request status and dates. When your access is approved, we email you from contact@lingonect.com
  • Visible only to the Lingonect® administrator
  • Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (art. 6(1)(b))
  • Kept until you delete your account; a revoked request is kept so the revocation stays effective

Profile

  • Your username, native and foreign languages with levels, certification status, plan and the date your profile was created
  • Visible to all signed-in members (for example in the chat user list). Your email address is never shown to other members
  • Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (art. 6(1)(b))
  • Kept until you change it or delete your account

Chat

  • Messages you send (text, your username and time), chat invitations, and, while you use the chat, your online status, username, first language and last-seen time, which other members can see
  • Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (art. 6(1)(b))
  • Messages are deleted automatically 24 hours after they are sent

Flashcards and search history

  • The content of your flashcard stacks
  • For registered users, a search history: each search's text, languages, methodology, date and time, plus a short summary (max 150 words) of the results — never the full output. Only the 100 most recent searches are kept; older ones are deleted automatically. You can download it via the “History” link on your Profile page
  • Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (art. 6(1)(b))
  • Kept until you delete them or your account

Text you translate

  • Text you submit is sent to the translation and AI engines you select (see section 4) to produce the results. If you use the apps without an account, it is not stored by us
  • When you rate translations, we store the ratings, the languages and the first 200 characters of the source text, without your name or account. These anonymous ratings are kept to build the public engine rankings
  • Please do not enter confidential information or personal data about yourself or others

Usage and security data

  • Each request to the translation engines is logged with your account ID, email address, IP address, country, the website or app it came from, the engine model and the request size
  • Used to prevent abuse, enforce usage limits and control costs
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and affordable (art. 6(1)(f))
  • Request logs are kept by our hosting provider for a few days; daily usage counters (per account and per IP address) for 2 days; the total request count of a free account for as long as the account exists

On your device

  • Your browser or the app stores what is needed to keep you signed in, plus a few display preferences
  • We use no advertising or analytics cookies and no tracking

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling
  • We do not make automated decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects

4. Service Providers and International Transfers

We rely on the following providers, who process data on our behalf or receive it to deliver the service:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore stored in London, United Kingdom; Realtime Database for chat status, stored in the United States; Google Workspace for email)
  • Cloudflare (runs the connections to the translation engines and keeps the request logs described above)
  • Translation and AI engines. The text you submit and the chosen languages are sent to each engine you select; your name and email address are not. These providers are based in the European Union (DeepL, Mistral), the United States (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta via Together AI, xAI, Perplexity), China (Alibaba, Baidu, Tencent, ByteDance, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Zhipu), South Korea (Naver), Russia (Yandex), India (Sarvam), the United Arab Emirates (TII, Inception) and Qatar (Fanar)

Some of these countries are not recognised by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection. Your text is sent to an engine only because you ask that engine for a translation, and each provider handles it under its own terms, which Lingonect® does not control. If this is a concern, select only engines based in the EU or in countries with an adequacy decision, and do not submit personal data.

5. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time on your Profile page (“Delete My Account”). This immediately removes your account, profile, username, flashcards, search history, chat messages and chat status, and your access request. Exceptions: a revoked access request is kept (see section 2), anonymous ratings stay because they cannot be linked to you, and request logs and usage counters expire on their own as described above.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access your data and receive a copy
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted
  • Restrict processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interest

To exercise these rights, email contact@lingonect.com. We reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Spain the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es), or the authority in the EU country where you live.

7. Children

Lingonect® is not intended for children under 14. If you believe a child under 14 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access rules that limit each piece of data to the people who need it. However, no system can be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed; for significant changes we will also tell registered users by email or on the site.
